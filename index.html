    FAQ

    Questions

    Is Mirai live?

    The board and the chart use live Robinhood Chain token prices and historical candles. You can connect an injected wallet (MetaMask, OKX Wallet, and others) to Robinhood Chain. Connecting never sends a transaction. The ticket, comparison slider, swap card, and pools list remain a preview: no order is routed, no swap is sent, and no deposit is taken.

    Does Swap or Pools trade?

    No. ETH is priced from the deepest live WETH/USDG pool on Robinhood Chain. USDC has no address in the page config, so it is shown at one dollar and cannot be moved from here. MIRAI is marked CA: soon. The swap button reads Trading opens at launch whenever MIRAI is selected, and it never sends a transaction. Pool TVL, volume, fees, and APR stay blank until a real pool exists.

    If I buy a stock token, do I own the share?

    No. A stock token tracks or references a listed name. It does not confer share ownership, voting rights, dividends, or any claim on the issuer.

    How does the privacy model work?

    You can read your own balances, positions, and receipts. A public observer sees that markets exist and, at most, an anonymized receipt hash — not your identity, size, or side. This page illustrates that idea; it is not a live cryptographic system.

    What leverage is shown here?

    Modest gearing only: 1×, 1.5×, or 2×, long or short, and only in the perps ticket. A 2× long is sketched as liquidated after a decline of roughly half, less a small maintenance cushion.

    What fee does the ticket use?

    Ten basis points (0.10%) on the spot notional, or on the leveraged notional for perps. That rate is a preview assumption, not a posted live fee schedule.

    Has the protocol been audited?

    No. Mirai, as presented here, has not been audited. Do not treat this page as production software or as a safe venue for funds.

    What chain is this built for?

    Robinhood Chain. Wallet connection, deposits, and settlement — when they exist — are intended for that network, including any testnet used for trials.

    A stock token is not share ownership. Leverage can extinguish your entire margin, including on a modest 2× position. A testnet or demo book may be reset, delayed, or wrong.
